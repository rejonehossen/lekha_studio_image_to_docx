About {{ PRODUCT_NAME }}

{{ PRODUCT_NAME }} is a local-first Bangla & English OCR publishing studio. Upload images, extract text with Tesseract, edit it in place, and generate real, editable Microsoft Word documents with professional typography.

This application runs entirely on your machine. It has no login, no user accounts, no REST API, and no external services. Data is stored in SQLite and on your local filesystem.

Technical summary

  • Django (one app: studio, one custom model: Project)
  • SQLite database
  • Tesseract OCR
  • python-docx for DOCX export

Honest scope

Handwriting Bangla recognition is not provided. Fonts are named in the DOCX but not embedded. Word rendering may reflow pages across different office applications.
